Past conversations and other devices (optional)

DRAFT wording: to be replaced with the IRB-approved consent text before release.

With your permission, Advice Tracker also reads your conversation list on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok and Perplexity, in this browser, while a page of the site is open. It reads it through the site's own web interface, as the site's own pages do, at most once every minute.

  • Past conversations: conversations from up to 90 days before you joined the study. When one looks like you asked for advice, you are invited (on that site's page, at most twice a day) to answer questions about it in a separate tab. You can earn $2 for each past conversation you annotate, up to 20 ($40); each takes about 5-10 minutes. Answering No or Skip is always fine and never affects your participation or your base payment.
  • Other devices: conversations you have after joining in the sites' phone or desktop apps, or in another browser (they appear in the website's conversation list too). When one looks like advice, you are asked about it as about those on the web; if you continue it on the web, it is asked about there.

Your messages are checked by the study's own AI model, with names and other personal details removed first; it keeps only a yes or no. A conversation's text is sent to the study only together with your answers about it, with personal details removed. Messages sent while tracking was paused, on a site you excluded, on days the extension was not running, or before you last used "Delete all data", are never read. To keep a conversation out, pause tracking (turning the extension off does not keep out what you type on your phone meanwhile). Only the account you are signed in with the first time is read, never a work or team workspace (on Grok, which does not say which account is signed in, one account in this browser is assumed, and reading stops while the conversation list looks like someone else's). You can turn each option off here at any time: the extension then stops reading, and deletes what it found that you did not answer. Note for the study team: reading conversation lists automatically may not be allowed by every site's terms of use.
