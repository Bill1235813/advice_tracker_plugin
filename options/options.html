Advanced (for the research team; do not change unless asked)
Nothing is recorded while tracking is paused, and the study's rules count the pause: under 12 hours, nothing happens; from 12 to 24 hours, you get a reminder and your payment is not affected; from 24 hours to 1 week, your payment for that week is reduced for the time paused; over 1 week, your participation in the study ends. Study rules

Past conversations and other devices

Advice Tracker also reads your conversation list on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok and Perplexity, in this browser, while a page of the site is open. It reads it through the site's own web interface, as the site's own pages do, at most once every minute.

  • Other devices (part of the study): conversations you have after joining in the sites' phone or desktop apps, or in another browser (they appear in the website's conversation list too). When one looks like advice, you are asked about it as about those on the web; if you continue it on the web, it is asked about there.
  • Past conversations (optional): conversations from up to 90 days before you joined the study. When one looks like you asked for advice, you are invited (on that site's page, at most twice a day) to answer questions about it in a separate tab. You can earn $2 for each past conversation you annotate, up to 30 ($60); each takes about 4 to 8 minutes. Answering No (it was not advice: the conversation is then sent with your No, with personal details removed) or Skip (you do not want to annotate it: nothing of it is sent) is always fine and never affects your participation or your base payment. The questions about past conversations are due by the end of the study (week 5); please answer them early, while you remember the details. The study team reviews the answers: an annotation of low quality is removed and not paid.

Your messages are checked by the study's own AI model, with names and other personal details removed first; it keeps only a yes or no. A conversation's text is sent to the study only together with your answers about it, or with your No when you answer that it was not advice (so the study can see what its model got wrong), in both cases with personal details removed. Messages sent while tracking was paused, on a site you excluded, on days the extension was not running, or before you last used "Delete all data", are never read. To keep a conversation out, pause tracking (turning the extension off does not keep out what you type on your phone meanwhile). On each site, only the account you are signed in with the first time is read, whether it is a personal one or a work, school or team one; while another account or workspace is open there, reading that site stops (on Grok, which does not say which account is signed in, one account in this browser is assumed, and reading stops while the conversation list looks like someone else's). You can turn past conversations off here at any time: the extension then stops reading them, and deletes those it found that you did not annotate.

Test timings (test build only)

Type a time as a number and a unit: 30s, 5m, 2h or 7d (decimals allowed). An empty field keeps the time shown grey in it: the study's, and for the times marked * as many study days or hours as the study says, so they follow “One study day lasts” (with 5m, the five weeks last about 3 hours). Press Save: every timer then starts again with the new times. Once a test time is set, they also replace the follow-up and quiet times under Advanced.
