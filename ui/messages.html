Messages from the study team

At the end of a study week the extension reminds you here if you were not active or have due tasks left, and says by when to finish. The study team can also send you a message here, for example a request to update the extension. A new one also shows as a notification and in a small window on your AI chat pages, until you click OK, I have read it. To answer one, click the Advice Tracker icon, then Send feedback on the study, or send us a message on Prolific.
