Free support resources

If thinking about this decision is upsetting, these free services can help:

  • 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline: call or text 988, or chat at 988lifeline.org (free, 24 hours a day)
  • Crisis Text Line: text HOME to 741741 (free, 24 hours a day)
  • SAMHSA National Helpline: call 1-800-662-4357 (free and confidential, 24 hours a day, for mental health and substance use)

If you are in danger right now, call 911. AI chat assistants are not doctors, therapists, lawyers or financial advisers. For important decisions, please also talk to a professional.
