Questions about the conversations you are having now: quick check-ins

Your advice conversations

From a week after you asked the AI for advice, we ask whether you made a decision and acted on it, then how it turned out. Everything you have not answered yet stays listed here: conversations you have not decided about, decisions you have not acted on, and outcomes that are not answered yet or were too early to tell. While anything is listed, a reminder comes every 2 days.

With the outcome questions, this page shows you the conversation (your messages and the AI's replies) with names, e-mail addresses, phone numbers and similar details already removed. You can delete anything else you do not want us to keep. The conversation is then stored on our study server with your answers.

In the last week of your study, the outcome questions of every conversation are due by the end of the study, even if you have not acted yet or it is too early to tell. Study rules: payments and participation

Past conversations

The questions about past conversations are due by the end of your study. Please answer them early: the longer you wait, the more details you forget, and annotations of low quality are removed by the study team and not paid. A past conversation you do not want to annotate can be skipped: nothing is due for it then. A reminder comes every 2 days while past conversations wait here, and none once all are answered. You review a past conversation in the same way: its questions show it with personal details already removed, and you can delete anything else you do not want us to keep.

Free support resources

If thinking about this decision is upsetting, these free services can help:

  • 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline: call or text 988, or chat at 988lifeline.org (free, 24 hours a day)
  • Crisis Text Line: text HOME to 741741 (free, 24 hours a day)
  • SAMHSA National Helpline: call 1-800-662-4357 (free and confidential, 24 hours a day, for mental health and substance use)

If you are in danger right now, call 911. AI chat assistants are not doctors, therapists, lawyers or financial advisers. For important decisions, please also talk to a professional.
