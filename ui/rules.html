What you are paid

  • $5 for the consent and the first questionnaire.
  • $10 for each of weeks 1 to 3, paid 5 days after the end of the week.
  • $10 for weeks 4 and 5 together, paid 5 days after the end of week 5.
  • $5 if you take part for all five weeks and complete the final questionnaire.
  • $10 high-quality bonus at the end of the study if your answers are of high quality. It does not depend on how many advice conversations you had.
  • $2 for each past conversation you answer the questions about on time, by the end of week 5 (optional, up to 30). It is paid with the next payment after you answer.

A payment is made when you were active during that time and completed all the due tasks. A week is each 7 days after you installed the extension.

  • Active: the extension stayed installed and turned on, and you did at least one of these: (1) had an advice conversation, (2) answered quick questions or follow-up questions, or (3) sent concrete feedback through the extension, for example about an advice conversation it missed or a possible improvement.
  • Due tasks: the quick questions for every advice conversation that started in weeks 1 to 3 and, in week 5, the outcome questions for all your advice conversations. In week 5 they must be answered even if you have not acted yet or it is too early to tell the outcome. (Before week 5 the outcome questions are not due tasks: the reminder of your Outcomes page every 2 days asks you to answer them as soon as you can.)

Reminder, not active or late, and quality review

  • Reminder. If at the end of a week you were not active or have due tasks left, we send you a reminder. You then have 3 days to (a) answer quick questions or follow-up questions that are waiting, if there are any, or send concrete feedback to stay active (an advice conversation started after the end of the week does not count), and (b) finish the due tasks. The extension shows this reminder by itself at the end of weeks 1, 2, 3 and 5, with the day by which to finish: as a notification, in a small window on your AI chat pages, and under the Advice Tracker icon (Messages from the study team).
  • Not active or late. If you are still not active at the end of the 3 days, you lose the payment for that week ($10). If you still have late answers at the end of the 3 days: for a week of weeks 1 to 3, you are paid in proportion to the advice conversations you answered on time (out of all your advice conversations of that week); for week 5, you are paid in proportion to the outcome questions you answered on time (out of all conversations after the start of the study). If you are not active or you have a late answer for any week, you do not receive the high-quality bonus.
  • Quality review. In the 2 days after that, we review the quality of the answers. We flag conversations that are clearly not advice but were answered as advice, answers that were filled in very fast, and other signs of low quality. We remove flagged conversations. If most of your answers in a week are removed, you lose the payment for that week ($10); a removed past conversation loses its $2.

Pausing

Nothing is recorded while tracking is paused. How long the pause lasts decides what happens:

  • Under 12 hours: nothing happens.
  • 12 to 24 hours: you get a reminder; your payment is not affected.
  • 24 hours to 1 week: your payment for that week is reduced for the time paused.
  • Over 1 week: your participation in the study ends.

When your participation ends early

  • You pause the extension for more than 1 week.
  • The extension detects very low use of the AI assistants, for example no use for a week. (It reports when you last sent a message to one of them: the time only, never the text.)
  • Nothing further is needed from you.

If you stop taking part or we end your participation, you keep the weekly payments and past-conversation payments you have already earned, and you are paid for the days of the current week ($1.43 per day in weeks 1 to 3, $0.71 per day in weeks 4 and 5). Your weekly payments do not depend on how many advice conversations you have, as long as you were active.

Your answers

  • Updating. When an advice conversation goes on, the extension asks whether to update your answers. Updating is encouraged, not required. We review the answers later: answers that clearly needed an update and were left as they were may be flagged as low quality.
  • Not advice. If the extension asks about a conversation in which you did not ask for advice, answer No. We keep that conversation, with personal details removed, to make the extension more accurate, and do not ask about it again.
  • Missed advice. If the extension misses a conversation in which you asked for advice, please tell us: click the Advice Tracker icon, then Report an advice conversation we missed.
  • Follow-ups. From 7 days after an advice conversation, the extension asks whether you made a decision and acted on it, then how it turned out. If it is too early to tell, say so, and it asks again 2 days later. Everything you have not answered yet is listed on your Outcomes page, and a reminder comes every 2 days while anything is listed there. In week 5 the outcome questions of every conversation are due. With the outcome questions, the Outcomes page shows you the conversation with names, e-mail addresses, phone numbers and similar details already removed; you can delete anything else you do not want us to keep.
  • Past conversations (optional). Their questions are due by the end of week 5. Please answer them early: the longer you wait, the more details you forget. A past conversation you do not want to annotate can be skipped (Skip this one): nothing is due for it then.
  • Messages from the study team. We may also send you a message through the extension, for example a request to update it. Like the reminder at the end of a week, it shows as a notification and in a small window on your AI chat pages, and stays under the Advice Tracker icon (Messages from the study team).
