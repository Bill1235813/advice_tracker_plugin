Study rules: payments and participation

What counts as personal advice?

You're asking AI for personal advice when you want guidance on what to do in your life. This can include making a decision, dealing with a problem, or working towards a goal.

Advice can be about anything personal in your life, such as health, money, relationships, work, school, or even habits, as long as it relates to your life. It can be about a specific situation or a general goal, and can be something you plan to act on soon or much later.

Quick rule of thumb: were you asking AI to help you figure out what to do in your own life?

Examples that count as asking for personal advice

  • "I got a job offer in Denver, but I'd have to move away from my family and leave a job I enjoy. Should I take the offer or stay?"
  • "My sister hasn't talked to me in a month. How do I bring it up?"
  • "Make me a weekly meal plan that's low in sodium"
  • "Here is my investing plan... Is it any good?"

Examples that do not count

  • Asking the AI to complete a task for you (often technical), such as writing an email, fixing code, or doing calculations, without asking for guidance.
  • Looking up information, without asking what to do.
  • Asking for general information or recommendations unrelated to a decision or goal in your life, such as "What are some good podcasts about history?"
  • Asking about fictional situations, games, roleplay, or imaginary scenarios.

What matters is whether you were looking for guidance about what to do, not whether you followed the AI's suggestions.

Free support resources

If thinking about this decision is upsetting, these free services can help:

  • 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline: call or text 988, or chat at 988lifeline.org (free, 24 hours a day)
  • Crisis Text Line: text HOME to 741741 (free, 24 hours a day)
  • SAMHSA National Helpline: call 1-800-662-4357 (free and confidential, 24 hours a day, for mental health and substance use)

If you are in danger right now, call 911. AI chat assistants are not doctors, therapists, lawyers or financial advisers. For important decisions, please also talk to a professional.
